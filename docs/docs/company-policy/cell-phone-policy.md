# Cell Phone Policy

Cell phone use is extremely limited and at times prohibited during work hours. You may be allowed to keep your phone on the production floor for emergencies.

If you need to take a personal call please take it off of the production floor and away from any meetings or clients. You may take your call in the break room or outside, ensure you let your supervisor know where you are going.

Please do not try to sneak cell phone use, we walk the production floor and will see it. 

Tech Leads, Managers and the Sales team all use cell phones for work related purposes, just because you see them using their phones does not allow free cell phone use on the production floor. 

If caught using cell phones repeatedly will result in disciplinary action:
1. Verbal Warning -  You will be asked to put the cell phone away and not to use it while on the production floor.
2. Written Warning - You will be provided with a written warning via print out and email. This warning will stay on your record.
3. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) -  You will be brought into a meeting and discuss cell phone use and how we can help you improve on this problem. It will be signed and reviewed. You will receive a printed and email copy of this PIP.
4. Loss of Position - Continued use after attempts to rectify problems can lead to loss of position. We do not want to lose our team members so help us help you before reaching this step.
