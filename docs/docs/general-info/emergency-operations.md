# Emergency Operations

## Building Issues

**For issues pertaining to the build please contact your direct supervisor.**
- Bathroom problems ie; flooding, clogged drains, non-working sink.
- Kitchen/breakroom problems ie; non-working sink, issues with fridge, broken cabinetry.
- Window broken.
- Door broken or not functioning correctly.
- Bay doors not functioning correctly.
- Broken and/or damaged wiring and/or plugs.

## Dangerous Weather Conditions

Safety of our team members is top priority please take this into consideration as everyone lives in different areas. Do not drive if you feel conditions are unsafe. We will send out emails if the building will be closed, please keep your contact details up to date to ensure you receive these updates in a timely manner.

**For dangerous weather conditions follow these guidelines.**
- If at work, check with your direct supervisor to see if any instructions have gone out.
- If not at work, check your email to check if the building is open during this time or if opening late.
- If no instructions have gone out, call or text your supervisor to check for instructions before driving through potentially dangerous weather.
If the building is open but your area has dangerous weather text your supervisor to inform and send email following attendance email guidelines.
