# Welcome

Welcome to **Bright World Technologies!** We are a global computer hardware refurbishment company with a world-wide presence spanning most continents, including Asia, Europe, Latin America and North America. Currently, we operate out of our home base in Dubai and right here in Dallas, Texas. Our Texas base is expanding rapidly and we are excited to have you as a member of our growing team.
