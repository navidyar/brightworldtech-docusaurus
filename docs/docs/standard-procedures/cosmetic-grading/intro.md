# Cosmetic Grading

Cosmetic grading is extremely important to our work. We need to be as accurate as possible. Our sales team and management use these records regularly and accurate data ensures information flows smoothly to our customers. 

The following are some examples of a units with cosmetic defects and what our technician input to document it. We need concise, yet accurate information.

- Discoloration and Scratches on Panel A, Dents on Panel D
- Discoloration & Dents on Panel A, White Spots on LCD Screen
- Discoloration & Scratches on Panel A and Keyboard Chasis
