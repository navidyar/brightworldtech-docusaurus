# Laptop Grading

Laptop Grading to be followed:

**Grade A:** Device may contain light scratching on screen or casing, light wear. No Dented corner. No worn or faded keys<br />
**Grade B:** Light scratch(es) on screen, minor dented corners, minor knick(s) or blemish(es), light Wear. Two or less light dead pixels. No worn or faded keys.<br />
**Grade C:** Moderate scratch(es) on screen. Moderate dented corner(s). Multiple knicks and/or blemishes. Multiple dead pixels. Moderate wear markings. Cracked Hinge. Worn or faded keys. Cracked bezel.<br />
**Grade D:** Deep Scratch(es) on screen. Major dented corners. Major dead pixels. Severe Wear. Cracked screen. Unit is functional<br />
**Grade F:** Non-Functional or *functionally defective device<br />


**Functional Defects**
- ⁠Swollen Battery
- ⁠Battery Health - 60%
- ⁠Damaged/Defective LCD
- ⁠Damaged/Missing Keys
- ⁠Logic Board Failure
- ⁠Damaged/Defective Trackpad
- ⁠Damaged/Defective Power Adapted
- ⁠Damaged/Defective Ports
- ⁠Defective Camera
- Missing Screws
- ⁠Non-Functioning Audio
- ⁠Non-Functioning Wifi
