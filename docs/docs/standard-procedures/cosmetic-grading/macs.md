# Macbook Grading

**Grade A:** Device may contain light scratching on screen or casing, light wear. No Dented corner. No worn or faded keys<br />
**Grade B:** Light scratch(es) on screen, minor dented corners, minor knick(s) or blemish(es), light Wear. Two or less light dead pixels. No worn or faded keys.<br />
**Grade C:** Moderate scratch(es) on screen. Moderate dented corner(s). Multiple knicks and/or blemishes. Multiple dead pixels. Moderate wear markings. Cracked Hinge. Worn or faded keys.<br />
**Grade D:** Deep Scratch(es) on screen. Major dented corners. Major dead pixels. Severe Wear. Unit is functional<br />
**Grade F:** Non-Functional or functionally defective device

## Functional Defects
- ⁠Swollen Battery
- ⁠Battery Health - Using Apple’s Published Standards
  - Intel based device: &lt; 900 cycles (Apple Standard &lt; 1000 cycles)
  - M1 & Newer: 80% or greater
- ⁠Damaged/Defective LCD
- ⁠Damaged/Missing Keys
- ⁠Logic Board Failure
- ⁠Damaged/Defective Trackpad
- ⁠Damaged/Defective Power Adapted
- ⁠Damaged/Defective Ports
- ⁠Defective Camera
- Missing Screws
- ⁠Missing/Defective RAM
- ⁠Non-Functioning Audio
- ⁠Non-Functioning Wifi
